Fees

Creator fees are split at launch. The creator's share goes to the wallet behind the account that asked for it. Pairoo keeps 20% to run the service.

Launch-only by design. Pairoobot creates tokens and answers questions. It cannot buy, sell, swap or transfer — those functions do not exist in its code. A capability limit, not a promise.

Questions

How does it work?

Mention @pairoobot with a token name, ticker and pair. It creates the token on Solana, trading against the tokenized asset you chose instead of SOL, and replies with the link.

What can I pair with?

The curated catalog above: tokenized stocks, gold and index pairs, plus pre-IPO names. Only assets from a verified issuer with real liquidity are listed — an unlisted ticker is refused rather than guessed.

Where does it work?

Telegram and X. Other channels are being rolled out and are not announced here until they answer.

Do I need a wallet first?

No. Start from the chat. Pairoobot never asks for a private key and cannot move your funds.
