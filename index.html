Questions.

How does it work?

Mention @pairoobot with a token name, ticker and pair. It launches your token on Solana, trading against the tokenized asset you chose instead of SOL, and replies with the link.

What can I pair with?

Tokenized stocks like NVIDIA, Tesla and Apple, commodities like gold, index pairs, and pre-IPO names such as OpenAI, Anthropic and SpaceX. Ask the bot for the full list.

Where does it work?

Discord, Telegram and X today. Instagram and TikTok mentions are being rolled out.

Do I need a wallet first?

No. Start from the chat — the bot walks you through what it needs. Pairoobot never asks for your private key and cannot move your funds.

What does it cost?

Creator fees are split 80% to you, 20% to Pairoo. Solana network fees apply to any on-chain action.
