  1. Send a request to privacy@pairoo.xyz with the subject "Pairoo — data deletion".
  2. State the social network, the username or ID and, if needed, links to the interactions, so the records can be located. Do not send a password, an access token, a full identity document or a private key.
  3. We will verify control of the account by an appropriate means. Do not provide another person's information without their authorisation.
  4. Manual handling must confirm receipt, scope, completion and any limitations. No automatic deadline is implemented; the operational deadline has to be defined by the controller.

The request covers the records under Pairoo's control: events, drafts, context and stored replies. Caches, AI history and backups must be handled as well, according to the confirmed inventory. Some records may need to be retained for legal reasons; any exception must be justified and communicated, never assumed.

Also revoke access in the connected-apps settings of the social network. Revocation does not replace a deletion request. Platform posts and blockchain transactions have their own controls; we cannot erase immutable blockchain data.
