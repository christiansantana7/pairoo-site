Privacy Policy — Pairoo

DRAFT FOR REVIEW — controller contact updated on 11 September 2026. The legal identification of the controller and the retention periods still depend on definition and implementation.

Service and data Pairoo is an AI assistant for social network interactions and for requests to create real blockchain tokens. The current stage of each integration is disclosed to the user; a simulation does not represent the creation of a token.

We receive public account identifiers and names, the text of comments and mentions, comment and post IDs, dates and processing states. Where a messaging integration is enabled, we also process the content and identifiers of those conversations. We store request drafts, proposed replies and delivery records to preserve context and avoid duplicates. Integration credentials are kept in protected storage, never on public pages.

Purposes and recipients We use this data to identify the interaction, prepare AI replies, fulfil requests, verify permissions, record outcomes and investigate failures. The text needed for this may be processed by OpenAI/Codex. The infrastructure includes Hostinger and OpenClaw; enabled integrations may use Meta, Ayrshare, TikAPI and Telegram. Providers may process data in other countries under their own terms. We do not claim that public comments, or data sent to third parties, remain private.

Retention and rights The current implementation keeps records in local databases for history, recovery and deduplication, with no automatic expiry routine configured. We do not promise a deletion deadline the system does not meet. Requests for access, correction, revocation or deletion should be sent through Telegram @pairoobot; execution is manual and requires proportionate verification of ownership, without asking for passwords or keys. The final policy must define the periods and align the backup configuration.

Disconnecting an account stops future access after revocation, but does not automatically erase earlier records. Deletion requests cover the records under our control; content on social networks is also subject to those platforms' own controls. Blockchain transactions that are eventually transmitted are public and cannot be erased by us.

Controller and contact The controller of Pairoo data is Pairoo Dev, the administrator of the service. Contact for privacy, exercise of rights and deletion requests: Telegram @pairoobot. No e-mail address is published yet. No company registration or legal entity is declared in this policy.
